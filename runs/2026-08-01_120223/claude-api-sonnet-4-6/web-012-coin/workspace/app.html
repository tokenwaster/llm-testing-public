Our Story

Forged in Obsession

WastedToken Mint was born from a single conviction: that digital culture deserves physical monuments. We take the ephemeral — the meme, the milestone, the moment — and press it into metal that will outlast hard drives, platforms, and market cycles.

Each coin begins as a hand-sculpted master die, passed through our proprietary multi-strike process to achieve depth and relief that machine-only production can never replicate. Our finishing team applies everything from mirror proof to antiqued patina by hand, one coin at a time.

We've partnered with communities, DAOs, game studios, and independent artists to produce limited runs that consistently become collector anchors within months of release. The token is only wasted if you don't hold it.

Craft

From Die to Desk

01

Design

Our artists sculpt every emblem digitally at 4× scale, ensuring sub-millimetre detail survives the reduction to die.

02

Strike

Triple-struck at 200 tonnes of pressure. Each blow deepens the relief and work-hardens the metal for edge crispness.

03

Plate

Electroplating in our in-house bath with real metal — no spray lacquer shortcuts. Thickness is guaranteed to spec.

04

Finish

Hand-polished, antiqed, or proof-buffed by our finishing technicians. Every coin is inspected under 10× magnification.
