About the Mint

Founded in 2024, WastedToken Mint specializes in crafting exceptional challenge coins that commemorate achievements, milestones, and camaraderie. Each coin is minted with precision, combining traditional metalworking with modern design, ensuring that every piece tells a unique story.

Our coins are more than currency—they're badges of honor, collectible treasures, and tokens of fellowship. Whether you're marking a personal victory, honoring a team, or building a collection, our challenge coins deliver the weight, finish, and detail that collectors demand.

Each edition is limited. Each surface is refined. Every coin is a statement.
