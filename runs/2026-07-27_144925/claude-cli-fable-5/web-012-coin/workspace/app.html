The Mint

We waste tokens so you don't have to.

Every WastedToken coin begins as a 38 mm, 3 mm-thick alloy blank, die-struck under 160 tonnes of pressure until the emblem stands in true raised relief — no printing, no stickers, no shortcuts. The rim is hand-reeded at 96 ridges, the same cadence the old mints used to defeat coin-clippers. Ours defeats boredom.

Each edition is struck once, serialized on the rim, then the dies are ceremonially retired. When a run sells out, it is out. We do not restrike. We do not apologize. We simply begin engraving the next season's dies.

A WastedToken is redeemable for exactly nothing — and that's the point. It is proof you were there, proof you belong, and legal tender only in the economy of respect. No blockchain was harmed in the making of these tokens.
