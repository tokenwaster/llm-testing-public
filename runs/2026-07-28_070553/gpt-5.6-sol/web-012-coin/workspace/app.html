The mint

Internet lore, made heavy.

Pixels disappear. Deadlines blur. A proper token stays on your desk and reminds everyone what actually happened.

WastedToken Mint turns digital milestones into tactile artifacts. Every edition begins as hand-built relief, then moves through high-pressure striking, edge reeding, antiquing, polishing, and obsessive inspection. The result is a coin with enough mass to feel consequential—and enough detail to reward the hundredth look.

We mint in deliberately small runs, number every piece, and retire each die when its edition closes. No quiet reprints. No infinite supply. Just enduring evidence for the people who were there.
