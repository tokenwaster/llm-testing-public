The Mint

A small workshop, a stubborn standard.

WastedToken Mint began as a private hobby and turned into an obsession. We strike collectible physical challenge coins for tight-knit crews, degenerate Discord servers, founder friend-groups, and a few institutions we are not allowed to name.

Every coin is die-struck in solid metal, never cast. We mill our own dies, hand-finish every edge, and number each piece with a small stamp that will outlive all of us. No two runs are alike. The metal is the message.

We do not produce thousands. We produce a few hundred, we ship them in velvet-lined boxes, and then we move on to the next alloy.

The Collection

The Process

From sketch to struck metal.

01

Concept

Every coin starts as a conversation. We work backwards from the meaning, never the metal.

02

Die work

Hand-cut steel dies, hardened and polished. The same way coinage has been made for centuries.

03

Strike

Solid metal blanks, struck under 12 tonnes of pressure. Each coin leaves the press hot.

04

Finish

Hand-burnished edges, inked relief, numbered, sealed in a velvet-lined case.
