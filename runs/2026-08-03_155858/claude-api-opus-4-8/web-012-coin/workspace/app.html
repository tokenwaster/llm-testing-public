The Mint

Where value is struck, not promised.

Founded in the ashes of a thousand expired wallets, WastedToken Mint believes a real collectible should have weight. Each coin is die-struck from certified alloy, hand-inspected, and stamped with a unique serial that ties your physical piece to its digital certificate of authenticity.

Our reeded edges aren't decoration — they're a promise. In the old days a milled edge proved no one had shaved value off the coin. We keep the tradition: what you hold is exactly what you were sold.

Every edition is capped, numbered, and retired forever once the run sells out. No re-mints. No dilution. When it's gone, it's history.
