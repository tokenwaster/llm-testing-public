About WastedToken Mint

For over three decades, we've been the premier mint of challenge coins for military units, corporate teams, and the world's most exclusive collectible communities. Every coin is hand-crafted with military-grade materials, precision die-striking technology, and an obsessive attention to detail that separates us from the rest.

Our coins aren't just metal—they're legacies in circulation. With reeded edges, deep relief embossing, and finishes that age like fine wine, each piece tells a story of achievement, camaraderie, and tradition. We don't mass-produce; we mint masterpieces.
