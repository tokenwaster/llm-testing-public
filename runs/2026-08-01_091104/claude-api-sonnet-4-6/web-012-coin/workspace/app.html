The Art of the Challenge Coin

WastedToken Mint was born from a single obsession: the belief that a coin should tell a story. Not merely a transaction, but a testament — to belonging, to achievement, to the moments worth carrying in your pocket for a lifetime.

Every die is hand-sculpted by our in-house artists. Every planchet is precision-alloy to within tolerances of a tenth of a millimetre. Every edge is machine-reeded in the tradition of the great mints. What you hold is not a trinket — it is a relic.

From our Denver forge to your hands, each WastedToken coin passes thirty-seven quality inspections. We mint in limited runs and never re-strike a sold-out edition. When it's gone, it's history.

Craftsmanship

From Concept to Coin

1

Design

Artists hand-sculpt each motif in proprietary CAD, reviewed across seventeen iterations minimum.

2

Die Cutting

Master dies are engraved by CNC to 0.01mm precision, then hand-finished by our journeyman engravers.

3

Striking

Each planchet is struck at 180 metric tonnes of force — twice — for crisp relief and full-metal flow.

4

Finish & Serial

Hand-numbered, edge-reeded, individually cased, and registered to a blockchain provenance record.
