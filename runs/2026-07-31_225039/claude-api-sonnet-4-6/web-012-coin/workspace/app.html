Minted to Last. Built to Mean Something.

WastedToken Mint was born from a simple conviction: the digital world moves too fast, and the things that matter deserve to be held. We craft premium collectible challenge coins that serve as physical proof of membership, achievement, and community.

Each coin passes through seven stages of production — die-striking, edge reeding, multi-pass plating, hand-finishing, quality inspection, serialisation, and archival packaging. We accept no shortcuts, no compromises, and no coins that don't make us proud.

Our collector community spans over 40 countries. Every edition is strictly limited. When they're gone, they're gone.

Craftsmanship

How We Make Them

01

Die Design

Our artists engrave master dies with 0.01mm precision tooling. Every relief, every texture, every letter — deliberate and exact. The die is the coin's soul.

02

Striking & Reeding

High-tonnage hydraulic presses strike each blank with up to 120 tonnes of force. Simultaneously, a collar die creates the characteristic reeded edge pattern.

03

Plating & Finish

Multi-pass electroplating — up to seven layers — builds the signature metallic lustre. Hand-polishing and antiquing brings depth and drama to every surface.
