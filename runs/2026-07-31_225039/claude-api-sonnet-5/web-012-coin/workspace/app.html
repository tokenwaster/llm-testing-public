The Mint Behind the Metal

WastedToken Mint was founded on a simple belief: digital achievement deserves an analog trophy. We take the tokens, ranks, and rites of the communities we serve and translate them into die-struck metal — the same process used by military and government mints, applied to the internet's most dedicated crews.

Every coin begins as a digital sculpt, is engraved into a hardened steel die by our toolmakers, and is struck under 45 tons of hydraulic pressure to bring the relief to life. No two runs are alike, and once a mintage is gone, the die is retired for good.
