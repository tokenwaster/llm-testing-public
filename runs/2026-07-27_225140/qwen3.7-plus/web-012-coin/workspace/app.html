The Mint

A small foundry for heavy ideas.

WastedToken Mint is an independent studio operating out of a converted boiler house on the edge of the city. We strike physical challenge coins for collectors, private clubs, and the occasional eccentric billionaire who believes money should have mass.

Every coin is die-struck from a master hub hand-engraved in our workshop, then tumble-finished, patinated, and serialized by hand. No two leave the bench looking identical — and that is the point.

We mint in small seasons. When an edition is gone, the dies are retired and the design is never struck again. What you hold is what will ever exist.

Each token carries a cryptographic provenance recorded on our private ledger — a quiet receipt that the coin in your pocket is the coin that left our bench. Scan the rim. Verify the weight. Trust the metal.
