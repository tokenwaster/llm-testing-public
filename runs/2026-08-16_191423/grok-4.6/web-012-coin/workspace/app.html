The Mint

We press the ephemeral into something that can bruise a palm.

WastedToken Mint is a private striking house. We take what the market evaporates — forgotten airdrops, memecoins that became campfire stories, leverage that learned gravity — and we cut it into challenge coins in the old military sense: a compact of belonging. You were there. You wasted well.

Every die is original. Fronts carry the Mint emblem in raised relief, a seal for those who still believe an object can outlast a ticker. Backs declare a denomination — the token count that earned the strike. Edges are reeded, seventy-two ridges, so a thumb knows it is not a toy.

No whitepaper. No roadmap. Alloys specified to the gram, serials logged in the house book, and a finish that throws a highlight like a knife. If it cannot catch the light, it does not leave the press.

Struck, not printed
