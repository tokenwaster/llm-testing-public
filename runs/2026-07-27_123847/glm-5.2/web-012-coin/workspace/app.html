About the Mint

We mint things you can actually hold.

WastedToken Mint began with a simple gripe: the most coveted tokens in crypto are infinitely duplicable strings. A JPEG of a punk is not a possession. A balance in a wallet is not an heirloom. So we went the other way entirely.

Every WastedToken is a struck-metal challenge coin — bronze, silver, gold, or obsidian — engraved with a denomination, sealed in a numbered capsule, and redeemable against the on-chain supply of the same name. The coin is the proof. No screenshots allowed.

Each edition is struck once. When the run sells out, the dies are destroyed in a live stream and the serial range is locked forever. What remains on the secondary market is the entire circulating supply — heavy, cold, and impossible to print more of.

How a coin is made

From artwork to annealed metal.

01 · Engrave

Die-cut by hand

A master engraver cuts the emblem into tool steel in positive relief. The die is then hardened and polished to a mirror — every line that will catch light is decided here.

02 · Strike

4.2 tonnes, twice

Each blank is annealed, weighed, and struck twice under 4.2 tonnes of pressure. The second strike brings up the full relief and the reeded edge in a single bite.

03 · Seal

Numbered & registered

Every coin is laser-serialised, sealed in an archival capsule, and its number is committed on-chain. The die is shattered on a live stream when the run closes.
