About WastedToken Mint

WastedToken Mint was founded in 2024 with a singular mission: to create the finest collectible challenge coins that celebrate community, achievement, and digital culture.

  • Authenticity Guaranteed: Every coin comes with a certificate of authenticity
  • Community Focused: Designed by collectors, for collectors
