🎱 Billiards

How to Play

  • Aim: Move your mouse over the white cue ball
  • Drag Back: Click and drag the cue ball backward to aim and set power
  • Shoot: Release to strike the ball (longer drag = harder shot)
  • Pockets: Six pockets: four corners and middle of each long edge
  • Reset: Click "Rack Balls" to return to starting position
