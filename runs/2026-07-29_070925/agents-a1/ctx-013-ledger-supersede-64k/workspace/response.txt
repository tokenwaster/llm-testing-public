To solve the problem we must simulate the ledger exactly as described: keep track of each transaction ID, its account and current amount (initial from SETTLED lines), apply VOID to remove it entirely, and AMEND to replace the amount when applicable. Only settled non‑voided transactions contribute; pending ones never count. Processing all entries in order yields the final balances per account, then we take the highest balance, lowest balance (lexicographically smallest ID on tie), total net sum over all accounts, and number of negative balances.
