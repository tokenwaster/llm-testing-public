The Art of the Challenge Coin

WastedToken Mint was founded on a singular belief: that digital achievements deserve physical commemoratives. Each coin in our collection is precision-struck using traditional numismatic processes — die-striking, antiquing, and hand-applied finishes — bridging the gap between blockchain culture and centuries-old minting craft.

We work with master die-sinkers, metallurgists, and digital artists to produce challenge coins that are as much works of art as they are tokens of value. Every edition is limited, every piece numbered, and every finish meticulously curated.
