Our Story

Forged in Tradition, Minted for the Bold

WastedToken Mint was born from a single conviction: that achievement deserves an artefact. Not a digital badge that disappears when the server goes down, but something cold, heavy, and permanent — a coin you can press into someone's palm and say this meant something.

We draw on centuries of numismatic craft — die-struck in solid alloy, machine-reeded edges, multi-step relief finishing — and combine it with modern laser-engraved dies to achieve detail that catches light in ways mass-produced coins simply cannot.

Each edition is a strictly numbered run. Once struck, that die is retired. The scarcity is real, the metal is real, and the weight in your hand is as honest as it gets.

Die-Struck Relief

Our hydraulic press strikes at 150 tonnes to push metal into every micron of the die, producing crisp, raised lettering and emblems that cast visible shadows.

Machine-Reeded Edges

A 120-groove reeded edge is applied on a separate operation, giving each coin that unmistakable heft and the satisfying ridged grip of a real numismatic piece.

Numbered & Certified

Every coin ships with a laser-engraved serial on the edge and a tamper-evident certificate. The ledger is public. The provenance is permanent.

How We Strike

From Concept to Coin

01

Design & Die

Our numismatic artists sculpt the obverse and reverse in 3D. Dies are CNC-machined then hand-chased to museum grade.

02

Alloy & Blank

Raw metal is alloyed to spec, rolled to thickness, and blanked to exact diameter. Each blank is annealed for optimal strike.

03

Strike & Reed

The press applies 150 tonnes in a single pass. Reeding is applied separately on a collar press to ensure consistent groove depth.

04

Finish & Plate

Surface finishing — polishing, antiquing, plating — is applied by hand in our workshop. Each coin passes 12-point QC inspection.
