ABOUT THE MINT

Struck, not printed.

WastedToken Mint is an independent art mint obsessed with weight, shine and lore. Each design begins as a hand-cut plaster, is digitised into a high-relief die, then struck twice under 120 tons of pressure. The result is a coin you feel before you see — sharp reeds, frosted devices, mirror fields.

We mint in tiny seasonal editions. Bronze for the bold, Silver for the sharp, Gold for the vault, Obsidian for the patient. Every coin is numbered, sealed, and backed by our melt-plus-mischief guarantee.
