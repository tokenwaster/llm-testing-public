Struck, Not Printed.

WastedToken Mint is a small, obsessive atelier dedicated to the ancient art of the challenge coin. Every piece we release is die-struck in solid bronze, sterling silver, or 24k-gold-plated brass, then hand-finished, antiqued, and numbered.

We mint in limited editions. When an edition is gone, the dies are destroyed. No re-strikes, no reprints, no apologies. What you hold in your hand is the only one that will ever exist in that state — a physical token of a moment that will not return.

Carry it. Trade it. Lose it at a bar in Berlin and have a story for the rest of your life.
