The Mint

Where digital honor becomes metal.

WastedToken Mint was founded on a simple conviction: the moments worth remembering deserve more than a screenshot. We take the tokens, milestones, and inside jokes that define a community and strike them into solid metal — coins built to outlast the server they came from.

Each edition begins as hand-cut die art, refined across a dozen proofs until the relief catches light exactly right. We mint in true bronze, sterling-plated silver, 24k gold vermeil, and — for the boldest collectors — a matte obsidian black finish reserved for limited seasonal drops.

No two runs are identical, no run is ever reopened once it closes, and every coin ships in a velvet-lined capsule with a numbered certificate of strike.
