01 / THE MINT

Artifacts for the terminally online.

Pixels disappear. Platforms pivot. A properly struck hunk of metal remains stubbornly, gloriously real.

We make collectible challenge coins for communities born on screens. Every WastedToken edition starts as hand-built relief artwork, is cut into hardened steel dies, struck under pressure, edge-reeded, inspected, and individually numbered. The result is equal parts membership badge, conversation piece, and pocket-sized monument to time extremely well wasted.
