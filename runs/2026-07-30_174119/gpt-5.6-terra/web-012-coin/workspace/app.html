WastedToken Mint turns magnificent detours, improbable victories, and high-grade nonsense into heirloom-weight physical currency. Our collectible challenge coins are designed in tiny runs, sculpted in relief, and struck with the kind of unnecessary care usually reserved for national treasure.

Each edition carries a denomination, a mint mark, and enough tactile detail to make your desk feel like a sovereign nation. No utility promised. Immense emotional collateral guaranteed.
