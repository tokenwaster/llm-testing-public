The Mint

We strike weight you can feel.

WastedToken Mint began in a back-room press with one stubborn belief: a collectible should have mass. Not a JPEG. Not a receipt. A coin — cold, dense, and reeded — that lands on the table with a sound.

Every edition is die-struck from a single procedural master, finished by hand, sealed in an archival capsule, and stamped with a serial that ties the physical piece to its token denomination. When an edition sells out, the dies are retired. No re-strikes, no apologies.

We mint by the season, in the open, and we count every coin out loud.
