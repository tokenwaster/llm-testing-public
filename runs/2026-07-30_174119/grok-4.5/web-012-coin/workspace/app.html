About the Mint

WastedToken Mint strikes physical challenge coins for digital fortunes already spent. Each piece is machined from solid metal, reeded by hand, and embossed with our mark — a permanent receipt for tokens that will never return.

We do not sell investment vehicles. We sell weight, edge, and the quiet satisfaction of holding something that outlasts a wallet seed phrase. Editions are finite. When a die is retired, it is destroyed.

Founded by collectors who watched portfolios evaporate and decided the only honest memorial was brass, silver, and gold you can flip across a table.
