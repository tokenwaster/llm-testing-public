The Mint

A small press, a heavy die, and an unreasonable amount of care.

We started in 2019 with one second-hand 60-tonne knuckle press and a stubborn belief that digital milestones deserve physical weight. Dies are cut from our own line art, coins are struck twice at full tonnage, then antiqued by hand so the relief reads from across a room.

Every edition is capped, numbered on the collar, and retired for good when the last coin ships. We do not re-strike. A mint that never stops minting is just a gift shop.

What arrives in the box

One coin, one letterpress certificate on 600 gsm cotton with your serial hand-inked, and a velvet sleeve nicer than it needs to be. The card lists alloy, strike date, press operator's initials and exact mintage.

Our one promise

If a coin arrives with a strike flaw we didn't intend, we replace it from the reserve and melt the flawed one on camera. The mintage number stays honest.

Process

Four days from steel to sleeve.

01 / DIE

Cut the steel

Line art is tuned for draft angles, engraved into hardened tool steel, heat-treated overnight.

02 / STRIKE

Hit it twice

Blanks are annealed and struck twice at 60 tonnes so relief fills into the corners.

03 / REED

Mill the edge

120 reeds rolled into the collar — the part nobody photographs and the first thing your thumb finds.

04 / FINISH

Antique & number

Oxide by hand, relieve the high points, laser the serial, seal, sleeve, ship.
