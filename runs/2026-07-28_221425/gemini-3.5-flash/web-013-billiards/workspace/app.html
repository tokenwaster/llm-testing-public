Retro Billiards 2D

Balls active: 16

How to Play:

  • Aim & Shoot: Press and hold the mouse left-click on/near the white cue ball, drag backwards to adjust angle and speed, then release to strike.
  • Power indicator: A colored ring and standard cue stick visual will guide your shot. A longer drag yields a harder shot.
  • Rules of Play: Shots can only be made when all balls are completely stopped.
  • If the cue ball is potted (scratch), it will automatically respawn at its starting location once all movement has stopped.
