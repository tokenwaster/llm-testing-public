Masterclass Craftsmanship

Our forge combines historical minting machinery with advanced 3D volumetric design models. By generating high-definition relief files, we mold die-stamps capable of applying 200 tons of localized pressure.

The result is a solid metal medallion of extreme depth and tactical satisfaction. Beautifully weighted, deeply personal, and visually spectacular.

Reeded Edge

Each blank is processed through standard security-reeded machinery, delivering a ridge count identical to physical bullion.

High Relief

Dual-Finish

10,000 Mint Weight
