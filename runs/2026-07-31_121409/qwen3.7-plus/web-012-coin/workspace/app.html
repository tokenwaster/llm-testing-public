The Mint

WastedToken Mint strikes premium physical challenge coins for collectors, communities, and the discerning few who understand that true value is something you can hold. Each piece is die-struck in limited editions, individually numbered, and certified authentic — carrying the weight of centuries-old minting tradition fused with unapologetic modern design.

From bronze strikes for the faithful to obsidian-black editions for the bold, every WastedToken is a tangible artifact of belonging. No blockchain. No screenshots. Just cold, heavy metal and the stories it carries.
