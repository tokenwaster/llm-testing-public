The Mint

Provenance Over Hype

Established 2021 in a decommissioned server farm outside Austin. We don't mint tokens — we mint artifacts. Every coin bears the weight of intention: cold-struck from certified alloys, serial-numbered, and vaulted until they reach your hand.

Zero Digital Promises

No roadmaps. No utility. No discord roles. A WastedToken coin is valuable because it exists — heavy, tangible, finite. The blockchain records the mint; the metal proves it.

Chain of Custody

Each edition ships in a tamper-evident acrylic capsule with a certificate of authenticity. The mint ledger is public, immutable, and auditable. You're not buying entry — you're buying exit from the ephemeral.
