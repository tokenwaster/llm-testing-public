🎱 POCKET BILLIARDS

Break when ready!
How to shoot: press on the white cue ball, drag backwards (away from your target) to set direction & power — the longer the drag, the harder the shot — then release to strike. An aim line and power meter appear while you drag. Pot the coloured balls into any of the 6 pockets (4 corners + 2 in the middle of the long rails). If the cue ball is potted it is re-spotted automatically (or click a clear spot to place it yourself). Use Reset / Rack to return all balls to the starting layout.
