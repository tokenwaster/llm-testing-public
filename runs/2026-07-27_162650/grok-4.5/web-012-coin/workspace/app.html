About the Mint

WastedToken Mint forges collectible challenge coins for those who treat every gas fee as a campaign ribbon. Each piece is struck in small runs, inspected by hand, and serialized against a public ledger of provenance.

Our dies carry the house emblem — a laurel of burning gas and a voided token — embossed in deep relief so light rakes across every ridge. The reverse states the denomination in plain metal: how many tokens were wasted to earn the right to hold one.

No app required. No seed phrase. Just cold metal that clinks when you set it down on a mahogany desk and pretend the market never dumped.
