About WastedToken Mint

Founded in 2019 by a collective of numismatists, cryptographers, and metalworkers, WastedToken Mint bridges the tactile gravity of traditional challenge coins with the verifiable scarcity of on-chain assets. Every edition is struck in our Denver facility using century-old coining presses retrofitted with laser-etching arrays for micro-detail work.

We mint in small batches — typically 500 to 2,000 pieces per edition — with each coin bearing a unique serial number lasered into the reeded edge and a corresponding NFT recorded on Ethereum L2. No pre-sales, no whitelists, no artificial scarcity. When an edition sells out, the dies are destroyed.

Collectors receive a certificate of authenticity, a tamper-evident acrylic capsule, and the option to vault the physical coin in our climate-controlled depository while holding the digital twin. Redemption burns the NFT. Your coin, your rules.
