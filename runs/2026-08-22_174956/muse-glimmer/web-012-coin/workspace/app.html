About the Mint

WastedToken Mint produces limited-run challenge coins for collectors, crews and the chronically online. Each piece is struck from a single blank, hand-finished and serialized. The front bears the WastedToken emblem in raised relief; the reverse declares the denomination in tokens. No two seasons repeat. If you see it, you can hold it. If it’s gone, it’s gone.
