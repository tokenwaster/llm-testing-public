The Mint

Where effort goes to be memorialised.

Founded in 2019 on a simple, sincere principle: the most valuable thing you own is time — so we cast a small, heavy monument to the parts of it you burned magnificently.

Every WastedToken is die-struck to numismatic tolerances: 40 mm across, 3.2 mm thick, with a hand-tuned reeded edge of 120 ridges. Faces are embossed in genuine raised relief — no printing, no stickers, no shortcuts. The obverse bears the Mint emblem; the reverse denominates your loss in TOKENS, the only currency that never appreciates.

We mint in strictly numbered seasons and destroy the dies afterward. What's gone is gone — a philosophy we take, perhaps, too literally.
