About the Mint

WastedToken Mint crafts heirloom-quality challenge coins for elite communities, corporate milestones, and special operations teams worldwide. Each coin is struck from precision-engraved dies, hand-finished, and serialized on-chain for irrefutable provenance.

Our master engravers combine decades of minting artistry with modern digital verification — every coin carries a cryptographic token embedded at the moment of creation. Collect, trade, and display with absolute confidence.

From bronze to obsidian, every edition is a limited run. Once a season closes, the dies are destroyed. No re-strikes. No reprints. Only legacy.
