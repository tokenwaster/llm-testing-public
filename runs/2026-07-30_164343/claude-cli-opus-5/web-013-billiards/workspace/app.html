Billiards

How to shoot: press the mouse on the white cue ball, drag backwards (away from where you want it to go) and release to strike. The further you drag back, the harder the shot — the aim line and power bar show your shot while you drag. Release inside the ball (or press Esc) to cancel. Sink the coloured balls in the six pockets; if the cue ball is potted it is spotted again. Use Reset / Rack to re-rack at any time.
