About the Mint

We turn wasted tokens into things worth keeping.

WastedToken Mint strikes physical challenge coins the old way — one die, one blank, one honest thunk of the press. Every coin carries the mint's raised emblem on the obverse and a denominated token count on the reverse, so the thing that lived only as a number finally has a weight in your hand.

Each edition is capped, hand-numbered on the edge, and sealed with a capsule and a certificate of authenticity. No re-strikes, no secondary runs. When a season sells out, the die is retired for good — which is exactly why collectors keep the boxes.
