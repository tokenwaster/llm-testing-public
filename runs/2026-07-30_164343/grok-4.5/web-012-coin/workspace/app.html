About the Mint

WastedToken Mint was founded on a simple heresy: the most hard-won tokens in any game deserve more than a row in a database. They deserve mass, edge reeding, and the quiet satisfaction of flipping metal between your fingers.

Every edition is designed in-house, cut on precision dies, and struck in limited runs. Front faces carry our raised WastedToken emblem — a laurel-ringed mark of deliberate excess. Back faces declare denomination in unapologetic token counts.

Collectors keep them on desks, in display cases, and occasionally as high-stakes coasters. We do not judge. We only mint.
