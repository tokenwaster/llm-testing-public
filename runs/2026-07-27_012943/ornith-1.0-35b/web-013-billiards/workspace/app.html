🎱 Billiards Game

Drag on the cue ball to aim and shoot!

How to Play:

  • Aim & Shoot: Click and drag on the white cue ball. Drag backwards (away from target) to set direction and power.
  • Power: Longer drag = harder shot. A power indicator will show your shot strength.
  • Aim Line: While dragging, you'll see a dotted line showing the direction your cue ball will travel.
  • Potting: Try to pot all the colored balls into the 6 pockets!
  • Reset: Click "Reset / Rack Balls" to reset the game and rack the balls in a triangle formation.
