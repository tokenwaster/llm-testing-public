Billiards — 2D physics sandbox & pool table

How to shoot: press the mouse on (or near) the white cue ball, then drag backwards — away from the direction you want to hit — and release to strike. The further you drag back, the harder the shot; a white aim line and ghost ball show where the cue ball will go. You can only shoot when every ball has come to rest. Keys: R reset / rack  ·  Space pause / resume. Pot balls in the six pockets; a potted cue ball is returned to the head spot.
