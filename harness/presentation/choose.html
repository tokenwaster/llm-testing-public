How this shortlist is made

Each candidate must have a scored result for every task in your selected work. Scores average every scored run per task, then average those tasks equally. Speed and API-cost picks require a mean score of at least 0.80 and complete timing or price evidence. Subscription and local costs do not compete with metered API prices.

GPU memory is the recorded peak for the tested setup, not a minimum hardware requirement or a fit guarantee. Context size and offload settings affect it. Models with no memory measurement are excluded when you set a memory limit. Repeated trials are disclosed, not assumed.
