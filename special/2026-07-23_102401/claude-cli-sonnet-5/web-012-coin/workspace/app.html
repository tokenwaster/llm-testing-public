The Mint

WastedToken Mint strikes physical challenge coins the old way: hand-tooled dies, deep relief, and an edge you can feel in the dark. We don't do flat stampings or soft-focus renders — every coin that leaves our press has weight, a ring when it drops on a table, and a story on both faces.

Founded by a small crew of engravers and collectors who got tired of coins that looked impressive in photos and dead in the hand, we mint in short, numbered runs and retire dies the moment a season closes. What you hold is what was made — nothing re-struck, nothing reissued.
