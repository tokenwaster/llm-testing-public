The Mint

We strike memory into metal.

WastedToken Mint was founded by a small crew of engravers, collectors and incorrigible degens who believed that the best tokens are the ones you can drop on a bar and hear ring. Every coin begins as a hand-sculpted relief, is tooled into a hardened die, and struck under 200 tonnes of pressure until the emblem stands proud of the field.

We mint in seasons, never on demand. When an edition sells out, the die is retired and defaced on camera — so the number on your coin is the number that will ever exist. No reprints, no restocks, no take-backs. Just honest metal with a good story.

Each coin ships in a felted capsule with a matched certificate, individually numbered against the public mint ledger. Hold it, flip it, gift it, challenge with it.
