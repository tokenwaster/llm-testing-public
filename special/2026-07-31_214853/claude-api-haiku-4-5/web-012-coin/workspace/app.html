About WastedToken Mint

Since 2019, WastedToken Mint has been crafting premium collectible challenge coins for communities, projects, and legends who demand physical proof of their digital legacy.

Each coin is precision-minted with hand-detailed dies, polished to a mirror finish, and treated with our proprietary anti-tarnish metallurgy. We believe in making the digital tangible — tokens you can hold, flip, and pass down.

From bronze to black obsidian, every edition tells a story. Welcome to the vault.
