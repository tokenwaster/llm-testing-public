The mint

A mint for the modern mistake.

WastedToken Mint was founded on a simple observation: the digital age handed everyone tokens and left no one anything to hold. We corrected that — with metal.

Every WastedToken begins life as an honest blank of bronze, silver, gold plate, or volcanic obsidian black. It is struck twice at 240 tonnes, hand-brushed, reeded on a machine older than our legal department, and inspected under raking light by people who take the joke deadly seriously.

Each coin is serialized, sealed, and shipped with a Certificate of Zero Utility. It will not appreciate. It will not unlock anything. It will sit on your desk, catch the light, and quietly ask better questions of your portfolio than your accountant ever has.
