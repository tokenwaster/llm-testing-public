The Mint

A coin is a promise
you can hold.

WastedToken Mint began the way all good monetary policy does — as a bar bet. In 2026 our founders, tired of achievements that evaporate when a server shuts down, decided the only durable ledger is metal. So we built a mint: real dies, real presses, real weight in your palm.

Every WastedToken coin starts as a 3.2-ounce brass blank, annealed and struck twice under 160 tonnes so the relief rises clean. The edge is reeded by hand-set knurl, the rim is serialized, and each face is tumble-burnished until the highlight rolls across it like sunrise over a vault door.

The denomination — 10,000 tokens — is deliberately, defiantly unspendable. That's the point. You didn't waste those tokens. You immortalized them.

“Paper remembers nothing. Servers remember until the invoice lapses. Brass remembers forever.”
— The Founders' Charter, Article I
  • 01
    Die-sunk relief

    Emblems are carved in negative into hardened steel, so every strike raises the design instead of printing it.

  • 02
    Double-struck faces

    Two strikes per blank. The first sets the geometry; the second sharpens the devices to proof crispness.

  • 03
    Reeded, serialized edge

    96 reeds around the circumference and an edge-lettered serial. No two coins share a number. Ever.

  • 04
    Seasonal runs

    Four editions a season, then the dies are ceremonially retired. When a run sells out, it stays sold out.
